# UniBlue coverage expansion for devices, services, and network isolation

25 November 2026 · UniBlue baseline `50547fc`

**Build a device inventory that understands bridges, a service inventory that records where each service was seen, and an isolation report that explains who can reach it.** A single camera illustrates why. ONVIF can identify its video role. The recorder can identify the model behind a private camera port. The switch and firewall can establish whether guests have a path to its management interface. Each answers a different operator question. The path and the site's intended policy together justify a firewall change. [ONVIF conformance][onvif] · [UniFi isolation][isolation]

The [searchable coverage index](discovery-coverage-index.md) names **344 integration domains** with upstream discovery or attachment signals and **304 additional local-network integrations** that merit review. It also lists **all 161 service package paths** imported by UniBlue's pinned Nerva dependency. The [earlier 228-entry backlog](uniblue-coverage-research.md#part-2--detection-catalog) remains a source of individual product cases. Use the catalog below to decide which observations to collect, what should raise an alert, and what an operator can change.

The proposed catalog covers [26 device families](#iot-devices-to-identify), [27 service families](#internet-and-lan-services-to-identify), and [50 network checks](#network-and-isolation-checks) covering isolation, configuration and operational health. The [implementation order](#what-to-ship-first) identifies the dependencies and acceptance criteria for each release.

## What counts as coverage

| Result shown to the operator | Minimum evidence | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Asset or child device identified | A role-specific observation. For an exact model, a model identifier or enrolled inventory with its source. | “Zigbee motion sensor, model supplied by the lighting bridge.” The bridge owns the LAN address. |
| Service advertised | A fresh, typed advertisement tied to an instance and observer network. | “IPP printing advertised to the guest VLAN.” A reflector may have forwarded the announcement. |
| Service observed | A protocol-aware observation, including source network, destination, transport, family and time. A port number alone remains a candidate. | “TLS observed on TCP 8443. Application unknown.” A named TLS virtual host can be recorded separately. |
| Configured access | The applicable route, translation, ordered policy, selectors, return-state handling and enforcement points are known. | “Guest → recorder administration permitted by policy X on IPv4.” |
| Observed access or excess permission | An authorized observer saw the endpoint from the relevant zone, or effective service configuration/audit records establish a named grant. | “Guest can reach the recorder login.” An anonymous viewer grant requires separate permission evidence. |
| Unknown or conflicting | Name the missing input, expired record, conflicting identity, or unsupported policy selector. | “Switch ACL unavailable for this model. Same-VLAN isolation unknown.” |

Preserve site, observer, interface, VLAN or zone, IP family, timestamp, expiry, transport, service role, confidence and evidence source. Distinguish an endpoint, its physical device, a bridged child, and a proxy that fronts multiple applications. Keep a bounded, allowlisted subset of metadata. Counts and role flags suffice for checks involving camera recordings, print jobs, occupancy sensors or mailboxes. Rotating MAC addresses, shared chip vendors, reverse proxies and DHCP lease reuse all make weak identifiers unsuitable as permanent asset keys. [DNS-SD][dnssd] · [mDNS expiry][mdns] · [MAC address randomization][macrand] · [Zeek service records][zeek]

## IoT devices to identify

At the time of review, the Zigbee2MQTT public device catalog listed over 5,800 devices. It supplies model vocabulary. The site's own coordinator supplies evidence that a given device is actually present. Use the same distinction for Z-Wave JS, CSA Matter products, ONVIF, and LIFX. ### Which discovery additions buy the most coverage

Home Assistant's pinned DNS-SD table contains 118 service types. UniBlue explicitly browses 49 names, including the meta-query. The two lists share 37 names. **81 upstream types are missing from the explicit browse list.** That measures a specific collection gap. A product may already be visible through SSDP, shared HTTP advertisements, another device check, or the UniFi controller. [Full type-by-type comparison](discovery-coverage-index.md#dns-sd-service-types)

| Candidate | Why it earns an early fixture |
|---|---|
| Matter operational, commissionable and Thread border-router advertisements | All three service types are absent from the explicit list. Keep commissioning mode, fabric and border-router identity distinct, and use IPv6-scoped records. Matter even advertises an operational type ending in `._tcp` while it can use UDP. UniBlue currently feeds advertised TCP-typed ports into its TCP planner, so a transport exception belongs in this fixture. [Matter discovery][matter] · [OpenThread][thread] · [Port planning from discovery](../internal/probe/discover.go) |
| Shelly, OpenEVSE and SolarEdge Modbus | The first two already have product checks. Add their missing discovery types so a known device can be identified without another device-wide port assumption. SolarEdge supplies a separate energy-system case. [Discovery index](discovery-coverage-index.md#dns-sd-service-types) · [Existing community checks](../checks/community) |
| Lutron, PowerView and NUT | These missing types cover lighting, blinds and backup power. Each has a useful role distinction between a bridge and its children or a monitor and an administrator. [Lutron][lutron] · [Network UPS Tools][nut] |
| HomeKit over UDP and other proxied records | Keep the advertising interface, responding host and service target distinct. A reflected or proxied record should never silently assign all child services to the repeater. [DNS-SD][dnssd] · [UniFi mDNS][reflect] |

## Internet and LAN services to identify

Use the same inventory for self-hosted services on a LAN and services a site intentionally publishes. Give every service a visible state such as observed protocol, advertised protocol, candidate from port, or unknown. **7,336** distinct service names appear in the IANA snapshot. It includes name-only registrations, historical assignments and multiple services that can share a transport endpoint. A useful completeness measure is the fraction of observed endpoints a particular UniBlue release correctly labels on a representative corpus. [IANA registry][iana] · [IETF port assignments][portsrfc] · [Registry counts](discovery-coverage-index.md#reproducing-the-counts)

Start with the [pinned upstream recognizers](discovery-coverage-index.md#service-recognition-supplied-by-nerva). Measure their behavior through UniBlue on default and advertised alternate ports. Add a new recognizer when fixtures demonstrate a gap. For UDP, dynamic ports, passive infrastructure protocols and encrypted traffic, use authorized gateway flows, Zeek service observations, service advertisements or owner configuration. A flow with unknown application still contributes a real transport endpoint. Record the observed peer as local or external. An outbound connection to a SaaS endpoint does not make the client a server. [Zeek service tracking][zeek] · [IPFIX][ipfix] · [UniBlue adapter](../internal/probe/nerva.go)

Of the 161 imported upstream service packages, 126 declare a TCP or TLS-over-TCP recognizer and 35 declare only UDP or SCTP. For example, the dependency contains a QUIC recognizer, but UniBlue's TCP adapter supplies no UDP observation for it. Give each transport its own status. The first new service feed I would add is an import of existing protocol-labeled connection logs. It can identify DNS, NTP and other UDP traffic on the monitored link without contacting the device. [Pinned transport breakdown](discovery-coverage-index.md#service-recognition-supplied-by-nerva) · [Zeek service tracking][zeek]

| Industrial, telecom and routing control | S7, EtherNet/IP, PROFINET, DNP3, IEC 104, HART-IP, BGP, OSPF, PIM, Diameter and SCTP signaling families. These have different transports, and some are link-local. | Make these deployment-specific inventory packs using configuration or existing passive telemetry. Alert on unexpected peers/paths against a supplied design, preserving legitimate adjacency and redundancy. [NIST OT guidance][ot] · [BGP operations][bgp] · [Pinned recognizers][nerva] |
| Collaboration, streaming, gaming and personal hosting | XMPP, IRC, Matrix, RTMP, DLNA, media servers, game servers, game query protocols and consoles. Look for distinctive protocol observations or owner manifests. | Separate the intended public chat/game/stream entry from moderators' consoles, storage and metrics. Scope those additional interfaces to approved sources. [UPnP standards][upnp] · [Earlier media cases](uniblue-coverage-research.md) |
| Peer-to-peer and blockchain | BitTorrent, IPFS, Lightning, Bitcoin and Ethereum peering, RPC, engine/validator APIs and node dashboards. Attach the observed purpose to each transport. | Reuse existing WAN classifications. Report a management/RPC service behind a forward declared as peering or a newly broadened source range. Preserve approved peering. [Current WAN rules](../rules/wan_exposure.yaml) |
| Proprietary, cloud-only and unidentified services | Allow “TCP service observed,” “UDP conversation,” “TLS application unknown,” “shared CDN,” and “owner-identified.” A new DNS name or autonomous system alone cannot identify an application or device model. | Open a review item when a declared policy changes or an unknown service becomes externally published. Retain uncertainty when encryption or a missing observer prevents identification. [Zeek service tracking][zeek] · [IANA registry][iana] |

### Internet services used by local devices

An owner may also ask which internet services a camera, TV or workstation *uses*. Give outbound dependencies their own inventory view. Useful categories include manufacturer control, firmware updates, time and DNS, identity/sign-in, push notifications, voice/video relay, cloud storage and backups, productivity SaaS, and shared content delivery. Assign a category only as specifically as the evidence permits.

| Evidence available | Attribution to show | Example decision |
|---|---|---|
| Device's configured destination plus matching local egress record | Named dependency and purpose with the device/configuration source | A camera's configured cloud recorder is reachable. Compare with the approved recording policy. |
| Vendor-published endpoint feed plus matching hostname and fresh connection evidence | Likely service family and dated feed revision | Microsoft 365 publishes endpoint sets and changes. A match can identify a service family. It does not establish the account, tenant or document accessed. [Microsoft endpoint service][m365] |
| Gateway application label or DNS response near a flow | Attributed by gateway or inferred from DNS, with time and observer | A TV contacted a streaming provider. Distinguish an actual flow from a cached DNS lookup, and retain ambiguity on shared addresses. [UniFi traffic logging][flows] |
| Shared hosting range, opaque HTTPS/QUIC, encrypted ClientHello or VPN endpoint | Hosting provider, encrypted service or tunnel peer, at the confidence available | CloudFront publishes infrastructure ranges. A range match cannot identify which customer's application was used. ECH can conceal the origin name. [CloudFront ranges][cdn] · [Encrypted ClientHello][ech] |

Prefer completed-flow metadata or owner configuration, with coarse time buckets, over storing browsing histories. UniFi's IPFIX export can be sampled, and collector visibility depends on gateway model and traffic path. Missing samples cannot certify that a prohibited flow never occurred. [UniFi traffic logging][flows]

## Network and isolation checks

An isolation finding should identify the **source, destination function, address family, enforcement point and change that restores the intended boundary**. “IoT VLAN exists” leaves unanswered whether two cameras can talk on the same switch, whether a guest can use IPv6 to reach the recorder, and whether an mDNS proxy publishes the recorder's name to guests. UniFi documents separate responsibilities for gateway policies, switch ACLs and Wi-Fi client isolation. [Network and client isolation][isolation] · [Switch ACLs][acl] · [Zone-based firewall][zbf]

The following are proposed checks. A **policy mismatch** needs declared intent, such as guest isolation, approved admin sources or a required service dependency. **Configuration evidence** can justify a configuration finding. Describe the path as *observed* only when an authorized observer at the relevant source saw it. A passing check names the boundary and conditions actually evaluated.

### Gateway policy and external access

| Check | Evidence and counterexample | Recommended fix |
|---|---|---|
| Effective guest/IoT/camera/DMZ → trusted access | Resolve ordered rules, default action, groups, ranges, schedules, network and zone, and new vs established traffic. An earlier deny or return-state rule can change the verdict. | Narrow the decisive allow to the specific controller, printer or recorder. Preserve required services and established replies. [Zone firewall][zbf] |
| Untrusted access to the gateway itself | Distinguish controller/console administration from gateway-provided DHCP, DNS, time and VPN. Record the destination service. | Scope administrative services to management clients, retaining required infrastructure access. [Zone firewall][zbf] |
| IPv4 and IPv6 policy parity | Compare the same source, service and destination across both families, including routed globally addressed hosts, prefix delegation and management endpoints. A global IPv6 address alone proves no inbound path. | Add the missing family-specific policy and re-evaluate on prefix changes. [IPv6 security][ipv6] · [Residential IPv6 filtering][cpe6] |
| NAT and forward translate to the wrong service | Match external interface, IP family, protocol, external port, translated IP/port, source restriction and actual service role. A UDP-only mapping does not publish TCP on the same number. | Correct or remove the specific mapping and check its effective firewall disposition. [UniFi port forwards][forward] |
| Forward points at a different device after lease reuse | Correlate destination address with current owner, earlier mapping and intended service. A powered-off destination alone is insufficient. | Reserve the intended asset address or retire the stale forward. [UniFi port forwards][forward] |
| External reachability overstated behind upstream NAT | Track WAN address scope, upstream NAT/CGNAT, concurrent WANs and any known external observation. Hairpin success establishes an internal path. | Label the known mapping and use upstream configuration or independent authorized evidence for internet reachability. [UniFi port forwards][forward] · [Hairpin NAT][hairpin] |
| UPnP, NAT-PMP or PCP mapping drift | Record the actual dynamic lease and expiry where the gateway exposes them, owner IP, target role and source allowance. Merely enabling UPnP is a different finding. | Remove the unnecessary mapping or restrict which segments can request it. Preserve approved console/game mappings. [UniFi UPnP][upnpui] |
| Public proxy and direct backend expose different controls | Join vhost/ingress declarations, real backend bindings and firewall path. An authenticated frontend can coexist with a guest-reachable backend. | Allow origin access only from the proxy or authenticated management clients. [HTTP semantics][http] |
| Outbound tunnel publishes an internal application | An owner-exported tunnel route links a hostname, backend and access policy. Traffic to a tunnel provider alone is circumstantial. | Require the intended access policy or remove that publication. [Cloudflare Tunnel][tunnel] |
| Site-to-site, remote-access and overlay VPNs cross trust zones | Evaluate approved prefixes, group grants, routed networks and firewall at both ends. Separate control-plane login, tunnel membership and access to a particular service. | Limit exported routes and grants to named roles. Include guest, contractor and admin VPNs separately. [Tailscale grants][tailscale] · [Site-to-site routes][vpn] |
| Failover sends traffic over an unintended WAN | Examine policy routes, kill switch, backup WAN and actual failover records. Some sites intentionally prefer Internet fallback. | Apply fail-closed behavior only to segments declared VPN-only and check the backup route. [UniFi policy routes][pbr] |
| Overlapping site/VPN subnets or asymmetric routing | Resolve route domains, longest-prefix selection, NAT and return path at both ends. Independent VRFs can legitimately reuse address space. | Renumber, segregate route domains, or supply explicit translation/return routing as appropriate. [Network instances][vrf] · [Site-to-site routes][vpn] |

### Switching, Wi-Fi and physical boundaries

| Check | Evidence and counterexample | Recommended fix |
|---|---|---|
| Native VLAN mismatch across a link | Compare both ends of a known LLDP/controller link. A single known end or unmanaged downstream switch leaves the far end unknown. | Align native VLANs after confirming management reachability. [Port VLAN assignment][trunk] |
| Required tagged VLAN missing on a trunk | Follow the physical path from SSID/edge port to its gateway or L3 switch. Compare the required tag with each trunk allowlist. | Add the missing tag on the first blocking link and preserve the management VLAN. [Port VLAN assignment][trunk] |
| Unnecessary tagged VLAN on an exposed edge or feeder | Extend UniBlue's perimeter findings through parent ports and downstream switches. Compare what the edge carries with intended use. An AP uplink may legitimately require multiple tags. | Set a single-purpose port to its access VLAN or prune unnecessary tags on the feeder. [UniBlue port analysis](../internal/risk/ports.go) · [Port VLAN assignment][trunk] |
| Switch-routed traffic bypasses a gateway-only ACL | Identify the inter-VLAN routing owner per VLAN. Check applicable switch ACL, device/model support, order and direction. | Put the policy on the device doing the routing or deliberately move that routing to the intended enforcement point. [UniFi switch ACLs][acl] |
| Same-VLAN Wi-Fi peer isolation | Distinguish two clients on the same AP from clients on different APs. Model SSID settings and applicable switch controls for both paths. | Enable the appropriate client isolation and additional enforcement where peer isolation is required. [UniFi isolation][isolation] |
| Same-VLAN wired and mixed wired/Wi-Fi peers | Trace the actual switch path, including devices on the same unmanaged downstream switch. Gateway policy has no opportunity to filter local switching. | Use supported switch ACLs, port isolation or separate VLANs. Mark unmanaged downstream paths unknown. [Switch ACLs][acl] |
| SSID association gives the wrong effective VLAN | Join AP, SSID, PPSK/RADIUS assignment and observed client VLAN. A shared SSID can legitimately map different clients to different VLANs. | Correct the affected key/group assignment or fallback network. Preserve authorized staff and guest mappings. [UniFi PPSK and RADIUS][ppsk] |
| Wired NAC or MAC-based VLAN fallback is too trusted | Compare 802.1X/MAC assignment, authorized endpoint and failure/fallback port profile. Account for phones with downstream PCs. | Put failures/unknown endpoints on the designated restricted VLAN. [UniFi wired assignment][wirednac] |
| Guest portal and pre-authentication access | Join hotspot pre-auth exceptions with routing and service identity. Intentional DNS, DHCP and portal dependencies are expected. | Narrow pre-auth access to required destinations. Recheck client-to-client access independently. [Zone firewall][zbf] · [UniFi isolation][isolation] |
| Rogue or duplicate SSID confidence | Extend current AP alerts with managed BSSID inventories, radio bands, known mesh/extenders, history and switch association. A matching SSID or MAC prefix alone is ambiguous. | Tag as suspected pending corroboration and identify the AP or wired port for investigation. [Current Wi-Fi checks](../internal/risk/unifi_posture.go) |
| STP root or topology changes unexpectedly | Compare root ID, bridge priorities, link roles and change history with the intended root. A blocked redundant port can be healthy. | Correct priorities or investigate the changed edge. Treat repeated churn or an unauthorized root as the finding. [UniFi STP][stp] |
| LAG, redundant uplinks and inferred loops | Correlate both ends’ link memberships, forwarding state and controller topology. Two cables or many MACs on a managed uplink can be expected. | Align aggregate membership, review an unintended bridge, and retain redundancy. [UniFi aggregation][lag] · [UniFi STP][stp] |
| Physical jack or power role changes | Correlate existing PoE/MAC events with expected external device, maintenance window, phone pass-through and downstream topology. | Investigate the named jack, disable an intentionally unused external port, or restore the correct profile. [Existing anomaly tracker](../internal/server/anomalies.go) |
| Controller, AP, switch or BMC management on a user path | Record the exact admin endpoint, source network and permitted maintenance route. A shared physical link can carry an isolated management VLAN. | Restrict management to named admin clients or jump hosts, preserving adoption, monitoring and recovery. [Switch VLAN assignment][trunk] · [Redfish][redfish] |


### Local infrastructure, dependencies and drift

| Check | Evidence and counterexample | Recommended fix |
|---|---|---|
| Unapproved DHCPv4 server or conflicting lease | Compare server identifiers and switch/relay location to the authorized set. Confirm overlapping address use over time. Approved HA pairs and relays are common. | Correct the rogue server or conflicting reservation. Configure guarding where switches support it. [UniFi DHCP][dhcp] · [Kea HA][kea] |
| Unapproved IPv6 router, prefix, DNS server or DHCPv6 server | Compare RA and DHCPv6 observations on each link against approved routers, prefixes and relays. Router redundancy and delegated-prefix renumbering are valid. | Investigate the ingress port and apply supported first-hop protections. Maintain all authorized routers and required multicast. [IPv6 operations][ipv6] · [DHCPv6-Shield][shield] |
| Duplicate or overlapping LAN address pools | Compare interface/VRF, subnet, DHCP pool boundaries, statics and exclusions. Scope comparisons to one routing domain and time window. | Remove unintended overlap and reserve infrastructure addresses outside conflicting allocations. [UniFi DHCP][dhcp] · [Network instances][vrf] |
| IPv6 broken by blanket blocking | Inspect policy for router/neighbor discovery where appropriate and required ICMPv6 errors, especially Packet Too Big. Match scope and direction. | Permit the necessary message classes while retaining inbound service restrictions. [ICMPv6 filtering][icmp6] |
| DNS/NTP/boot dependencies accidentally denied | Compare site-declared destinations with the effective policy and authorized failure logs. A periodically offline device may have no fresh flows. | Add the narrow required exception, including return traffic, for the affected segment. [DNS recursion][dns] · [NTP][ntp] |
| DNS or egress bypass of a restricted segment | Use DHCP-assigned resolvers, actual flow attribution and the site's permitted destinations. Public shared IPs and encrypted DNS can leave application identity unknown. | Correct resolver assignment or egress policy, retaining approved encrypted DNS, vendor updates and recovery channels. [MUD practice guide][mudguide] |
| mDNS reflector leaks service names | Compare reflector mode, VLAN scope and services to sharing intent. Model an advertisement separately from access to the advertised service. | Reflect only approved types between the intended networks, such as household-to-printer. [UniFi mDNS][reflect] |
| Authorized discovery works but the application path fails | E.g., a guest sees the approved printer but cannot reach its selected IPPS endpoint. Verify SRV target, port, address family and actual policy. | Preserve the advertisement and allow the intended print path only. [DNS-SD][dnssd] · [IPP Everywhere][ipp] |
| Camera, BMC, backup or facilities VLAN has excessive lateral access | Model explicit directed dependencies, such as viewer → NVR, NVR → cameras, monitoring → UPS, admin → BMC. Source and destination roles matter. | Scope rules to required peers and functions. Preserve DNS, time, updates and management exceptions. [UniFi isolation][isolation] · [NIST OT][ot] |
| Device egress departs from a verified manufacturer policy | Match traffic to a model-specific signed Manufacturer Usage Description (MUD) and site exceptions. Account for update windows and shared cloud backends. | Report the unexpected destination class and let the owner approve or restrict it. Unknown devices keep their own baseline. [MUD specification][mud] · [NIST implementation guide][mudguide] |
| Device is attached to multiple trust zones | Correlate two interfaces to the same enrolled device or verified bridge. A laptop with Wi-Fi and Ethernet does not automatically forward traffic. | Report dual attachment as inventory. Raise a transit-policy violation when routing/bridging or an attributable forwarded path is observed. [Network instances][vrf] · [UniFi isolation][isolation] |
| Firmware lifecycle, advisory or unsupported infrastructure | Use exact model, hardware rev, vendor firmware, support status and affected range. A version-unknown model, a marketed “vintage” device and an unsupported device have distinct meanings. | Prioritize an applicable advisory using known exploitation and path impact. Recommend the vendor update, mitigation or replacement supported by that evidence. [Ubiquiti lifecycle][lifecycle] · [CISA KEV][kev] |
| New asset, service or rule broadening after change | Diff stable identities, role, observer, family, access scope and effective policy. Expiry, randomized MACs, AP roaming and a missing controller feed can imitate change. | Show the prior and current state, timestamp and affected path. Close an incident on fresh contrary evidence or owner disposition. [MAC randomization][macrand] · [UniBlue state](../internal/risk/risk.go) |
| Incomplete site visibility | Per site, track data available for gateway policy, switch ACL, VLAN membership, WAN forwards, logs, bridge inventories and observers. Record controller version, API mode and timestamps. | Say which paths could be evaluated. Direct the operator to the specific missing read permission, observer or inventory source if it affects their decision. [UniBlue controller](../internal/unifi/client.go) · [UniFi ACL scope][acl] |

### Network health and recovery

Offer these as operational findings alongside security posture. Each needs observed impact, a documented site requirement, or a sustained trend. A single low-rate client, idle backup link or temporarily busy channel is routine.

| Check | Evidence and counterexample | Recommended fix |
|---|---|---|
| DHCP pool approaching exhaustion | Compare usable pool after exclusions/reservations with active leases and allocation failures over a busy period. Account for short-lived guests. | Resize the pool, correct reservation overlap, or adjust lease length to site turnover. [UniFi DHCP server][dhcpcfg] |
| Internal DNS sends a client to the wrong service | Compare the expected internal answer and IP family with the resolver assigned to that VLAN. Split DNS can deliberately differ from public DNS. | Fix the internal record or resolver assignment and recheck the affected client segment. [UniFi DNS records][localdns] |
| Address or certificate rotation breaks enterprise Wi-Fi | When an owner supplies RADIUS identity and managed client trust settings, compare configured server names, trust chain and expiry. Account for an intentional renewal overlap. | Renew the correct certificate and distribute trusted server settings in time. Do not advise disabling server validation. [EAP-TLS][eaptls] |
| AP channel and airtime congestion | Require sustained utilization/retries and impacted clients on the same radio. Distinguish interference, weak signal and a busy wired uplink. | Review channel, width and AP placement for the affected radio and confirm performance after the change. [UniFi Wi-Fi guidance][radio] |
| Unintended wireless mesh hop | Compare expected wired uplink with actual AP parent, recurring transitions and mesh configuration. A planned wireless extension is healthy. | Repair the wired uplink or correct the AP's mesh role. [UniFi wireless mesh][mesh] |
| Underperforming or flapping Ethernet uplink | Compare negotiated rate, supported peer capabilities, link transitions and error deltas. A 100 Mb/s camera may be operating at its designed speed. | Inspect the particular cable, transceiver and peer configuration before proposing a switch upgrade. [UniFi wired speeds][wiredspeed] |
| PoE shortfall affecting cameras, phones or APs | Combine supported PoE mode, switch/port capacity and denial or restart events. Idle power draw alone cannot establish peak demand. | Match the required PoE standard, redistribute loads or provision the affected switch's power budget. [UniFi PoE modes][poe] |
| Multicast loss or flooding on AV/IoT networks | Correlate AV receiver complaints or traffic with IGMP snooping, querier election and router ports on the affected VLAN. Multiple eligible queriers can be legitimate. | Correct the querier or multicast path for the documented design. Preserve discovery separately from the media stream. [UniFi switch multicast controls][switchsettings] |
| WAN failover misreports an outage | Compare SLA thresholds, targets, link metrics and observed failover. A dead verification target can coexist with a healthy circuit. | Use independent verification targets and tune the affected WAN policy. Keep a timeline of failover and recovery. [UniFi WAN failover][failover] |
| Critical network configuration lacks a recoverable backup | Read owner-provided backup status, last success, scope and most recent restore test, with separate freshness requirements. A recent backup alone does not prove restoration. | Restore to a spare or offline environment as part of a planned recovery test. UniBlue can report the most recent verified result. [UniFi backup scopes][backup] |

| Site observation | Verdict UniBlue should give | Action |
|---|---|---|
| A guest sees an IPPS announcement. Policy permits guest → printer IPPS and denies guest → printer administration. | Approved guest printing. Record separate discovery and application paths. | None if guest printing is intended. |
| A management observer sees Redfish. The guest IPv4 path is denied, but the gateway has an effective IPv6 allow from guest to that BMC. | BMC identified. Report a **configured guest-to-management path on IPv6** with the decisive policy. | Restrict the IPv6 rule to approved admins. Re-evaluate both families. |
| A privacy camera sits behind the recorder's PoE switch. The recorder's enrolled list supplies model and firmware. UniFi sees only the recorder. | One recorder and one child camera. Direct camera reachability from the reporting LAN is unknown. | Show the recorder policy and firmware review. No claim of direct camera exposure. |
| The site permits a UDP game forward. A TLS admin service uses the same internal port number on TCP. | The configured forward applies to the game. Admin WAN reachability remains a separate question. | Preserve the intended game forward. Check any TCP, proxy or IPv6 path independently. |
| An uplink loses telemetry for five minutes and the IP phone reappears with its attached PC. | Data gap followed by two expected peers. | Preserve the incident timeline without raising a cable-tamper incident solely from the MAC count. |

### Keep the catalog complete over time

- **Device coverage.** For each site show fresh local clients, clients with only a vendor hint, clients with a confirmed family/model, and children known through an enrolled bridge. Give each a denominator and a time window. Separate sleeping/unknown devices from recently seen ones. For device packs, count validated family/firmware combinations against the reviewed upstream candidates.
- **Service coverage.** Count unique observed endpoints by transport, family and observer. Report application-identified, protocol-only, ambiguous, and unknown. Measure performance on non-default ports, reverse proxies, private TLS, IPv6, UDP and shared services. Compare each dependency upgrade against the pinned protocol matrix.
- **Policy coverage.** Count the intended source/destination/function combinations for which the site has sufficient evidence. Show configured, observed and unknown cells separately. Surface stale controller data or inaccessible switch policy alongside the affected cells.
- **Quality and cost.** Record incorrect verdicts on negative fixtures, operator-confirmed false positives, duplicate findings, observation age and extra traffic per host/segment. Include two difficult negatives for each new family, such as a login page and a shared OEM advertisement. Keep closed ports and ordinary inventory out of the action queue.
- **Admission criteria.** A shipping check needs a stable identity, precise claim, required data, exceptions, operator action, primary reference and positive/negative fixtures. A new service advertisement can ship as inventory before its security checks mature. Update source attribution, check upstream terms for any redistributed data, and pin the input revision in the catalog.
